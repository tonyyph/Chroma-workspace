# Accessibility

Target: WCAG 2.1 AA where applicable on mobile.

- Respect system font scaling and avoid fixed text heights.
- Give actions and image/palette content meaningful labels and hints.
- Keep interactive targets at least 44 points.
- Preserve logical route and focus order.
- Provide text/status icons for selected, denied, failed, local, and private states.
- Use contrast-tested foregrounds for user-derived colors.
- Respect Reduce Motion in every purposeful animation.
- Do not auto-play audio; expose preview availability in text.

Critical flow tests cover accessible names for photo selection, palette
confirmation, pairing selection, save, timeline item, and retry.
